- **A remittance advice: the X12 820 and the EDIFACT REMADV** ([#149]). The
  one leg of procure-to-pay the mock could not carry: the supplier's view of
  the chain ended at the 810, and learned nothing of the payment. The mock,
  as the payee, now receives a remittance advice, checks it against the
  dictionary - `GET /_mock/dictionary/X12/820` and `/EDIFACT/REMADV` describe
  both - and answers with a 997 or CONTRL, so `no-ack` and the other
  behaviours apply to it as to any document. Beyond the syntax, two things
  are refused by name: an 820 used as a payment order (`BPR01` `D`, `P`, `U`
  or `X`), which is a bank's document, and a total that is not the sum of
  what was paid on each invoice - `BPR02` against the `RMR04` amounts, or the
  `MOA+12` total against each `DOC`'s. Each advice is filed under its trace
  number. A supplier the mock buys from is refused one: the mock pays it.
