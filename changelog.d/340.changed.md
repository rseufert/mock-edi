- **The clock is spelt as every `at` is** ([#340]). `clock.now` in
  `/_mock/state` and `clock` in the answer to `/_mock/advance` said
  `2026-10-02T08:12:33+00:00` where the timeline said `2026-10-02T08:12:33Z`
  for the same instant. Both now end in `Z` when the clock is in UTC, so the
  clock and the next event's `at` can be compared as strings. A mock started
  with an offset (`--start-at 2026-11-03T00:30:00+01:00`) still says that
  offset. Python's `datetime.fromisoformat` does not read the `Z` before
  3.11; a client that parsed the clock with it now needs what it already
  needs for an `at`.
